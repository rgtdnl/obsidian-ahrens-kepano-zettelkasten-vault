# notes session

A session ritual for turning KOReader highlights into literature notes and permanent notes, with Hermes as facilitator. The notes are yours: every sentence is one you wrote or rewrote. Hermes structures, checks, links, and asks; it does not think for you.

## the contract

- you bring: reading, reactions, claims, judgment
- hermes brings: structure, links, checks, questions, maintenance
- anything hermes drafts is labeled a draft; rewrite or reject it before it counts as yours
- any link hermes proposes is a proposal with a reason; approve or cut it
- the verbatim check runs on everything, including anything hermes writes

## highlight convention (foundational, settled 2026-08-10)

| marker | meaning | destination |
|---|---|---|
| red | permanent note candidate idea | lit note candidates checklist |
| yellow + [!note] margin note | supporting permanent idea note | supporting material that rises to a permanent candidate |
| yellow | supporting material: context, argument, evidence | lit note bullets in your own words |
| grey | quote | lit note verbatim block, kept short and selective |
| green | process/mechanics, references (further reading), word definitions | lit note context bullets, further-reading links, one-line definitions |
| [!note] margin note | your own elaboration, in your own words | always a permanent-note candidate; kept verbatim in the lit note under "my thoughts" |

Color mapping for leftovers in old exports: olive (vocabulary words) counts as green; cyan (emphasis) counts as yellow.

Colors dissolve once translated. They are a reading-time signal that makes the pipeline predictable, not metadata to preserve.

## lit note session

1. raw highlights file sits in 00 inbox (KOReader export, colour-coded)
2. you draft 5-15 bullets in your own words, one idea per line, locators where possible
3. you copy your [!note] margin comments in as "my thoughts"
4. hermes facilitation pass reports three things:
   - gaps: highlighted points absent from your draft
   - verbatim flags: passages too close to the book
   - template fixes: frontmatter, locators, candidates checklist
5. for each gap hermes asks, it does not write: "what does this mean to you?"
6. you approve; hermes files the note, moves candidates into the checklist

## permanent note session

1. you pick 2-6 candidates from the lit note checklist (margin notes + red + yellow-with-note)
2. you name each claim: the filename, written as a full sentence
3. you draft one rough paragraph per claim
4. hermes polishes surface only: full sentences, tightened structure, no new ideas
5. hermes flags missing ideas as suggestions; you accept or reject, never silently inserted
6. hermes proposes links: "this extends X because ...; this contradicts Y because ..."
7. you approve or cut each link
8. hermes runs the sweep: links resolve, no orphans, no near-duplicates, embed intact

## close the loop

- [ ] raw file deleted (rule: the source is one click away)
- [ ] candidates checked off in the lit note
- [ ] open loops recorded
- [ ] new permanent notes linked and filed

## the demo example (elaboration, p. 124 + 144)

- gap: red-flagged "the best-researched and most successful learning method is elaboration" is absent from the notes
- your turn: write one bullet in your own words, e.g. "of all the learning techniques studied, the one with the strongest evidence is elaboration: tying new material to what you already understand" (this passes the verbatim check)
- hermes then: verifies, proposes links (extends "writing is the medium of thinking", "one idea per note is the precondition for recombination"; raises the p. 124 caveat about isolated facts)
