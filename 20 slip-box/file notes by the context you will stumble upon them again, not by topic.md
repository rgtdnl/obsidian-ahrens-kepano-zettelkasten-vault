The old filing question is "Under which topic do I store this note?"; the new one is "In which context will I want to stumble upon it again?" Topics are the wrong organising principle because a single note can serve many contexts and because topics develop bottom up from the notes themselves, guided by interest rather than a pre-made plan. Luhmann's index pointed to one or two notes as entry points into a line of thought, a way in rather than a table of contents. Structure is grown from the notes, not imposed on them.
