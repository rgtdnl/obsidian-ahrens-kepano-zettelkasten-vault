Old-style filing asked which topic a note belongs to; the slip-box instead asks in what context the note should resurface later. Topics make a poor organising scheme because one note can serve many contexts and because topics grow upward from the notes themselves, driven by interest rather than by a plan drawn up in advance. Luhmann's index pointed to one or two notes as entry points into a line of thought: a doorway, not a table of contents. Structure grows out of the notes; it is not imposed on them.
