The best way to deal with complexity is to keep the structure simple and follow a few basic principles; simplicity of structure is what allows complexity to build up where it is wanted, on the content level. Sorting notes into ever more topics and sub-topics makes things look less complex at first but quickly becomes complicated, because every new note forces a decision about where it belongs. A simple, standardised structure, one place and one format for each note type, lets ideas accumulate into a critical mass and combine freely, which is exactly what the slip-box is for.
