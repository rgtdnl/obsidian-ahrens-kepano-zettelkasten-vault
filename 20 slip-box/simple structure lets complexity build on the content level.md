Complexity is best handled by keeping the structure simple and following a handful of basic principles; a simple frame is what lets complexity accumulate where it is wanted, on the content level. Sorting notes into more and more topics and sub-topics looks like simplification but soon turns into complication, since every new note demands a fresh decision about its shelf. A plain, standardised structure, one place and one format for each kind of note, lets ideas pile up into a critical mass and combine freely, which is precisely what the slip-box exists to do.
