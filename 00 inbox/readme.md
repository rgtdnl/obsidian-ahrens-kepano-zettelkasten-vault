# 00 Inbox: the processing queue

**Purpose:** one flat landing zone for everything not yet processed. No subfolders. What you see is what still needs work. An empty inbox is the goal state.

The book: fleeting notes are reminders of ideas, collected in one place, processed within a day or two.

## What lives here
- **Fleeting notes:** quick idea reminders. Format free. Meaningless after 2 days unprocessed.
- **Clippings:** web captures (articles, podcasts, videos). Source material, not knowledge yet.
- **Raw literature notes:** verbatim highlights and quotes. A staging area, not a final form.

## Rules
- Process everything within 1-2 days. Untouched fleeting notes decay into noise.
- One place only. No second notebook, no second app.

## Daily action (10-15 min)
1. Open the inbox. Read everything in it.
2. Fleeting note worth keeping? Turn it into a permanent note in 20 slip-box, then delete it here. Not worth it? Delete.
3. Clipping with an idea? Extract a permanent note, then delete the clipping.
4. Raw literature note? Translate it into a literature note in 10 literature, then delete the raw note.
5. End the day with an empty inbox.
